1. Read the poster

A frozen image model (SigLIP) turns your poster into 768 numbers that describe its layout, color and content.

2. Predict human judgment

Our regressors, trained on ratings from 60 judges in the GraphicDesignEvaluation dataset, predict each score.

3. Stays on your device

Everything runs in your browser, so your poster is never uploaded. It was trained on web graphics, so treat the scores as guidance.
